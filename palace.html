The Palace of the Och'Idoma, Otukpo

Since colonial administration, one palace at Otukpo has held Idoma land's twenty-two communities together under a single traditional council. Here's who sits on that throne today, who came before, and how to request a visit.

5th Och'Idoma · Paramount Ruler of the Idoma Nation

HRM Agaba'Idu Elaigwu Odogbo John

Ph.D · Chairman, Idoma Area Traditional Council

Installed as the 5th Och'Idoma on 30 June 2022, succeeding HRH Agabaidu Elias Ikoyi Obekpa. He was selected by Idoma kingmakers in December 2021 and presented to the Benue State Government before his coronation. Since taking the throne, he has led the Idoma Area Traditional Council from the palace at Otukpo, delivered annual New Year broadcasts to the Idoma nation, and convened gatherings such as the Idoma National Peace and Unity Symposium and a Royal Banquet in Abuja aimed at strengthening unity across Idoma communities.

In 2026 he inaugurated the boards steering the Idoma Division Centenary Plus Celebration, marking 103 years since the Idoma Division was formed in 1923 — describing the milestone as a moment to document Idoma heritage and chart the next century.
